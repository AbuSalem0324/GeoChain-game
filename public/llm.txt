# GeoChain

> GeoChain is a free online geography game at https://www.playgeochain.uk/. A mystery country or US state outline appears with no label. Name a neighbour to grow the chain. No ads, no login.

GeoChain is a free game you play in the browser. It is an online game and a geography game: identify places by their borders, not by typing the hidden name.

Modes:
- World Countries — chain countries that share a land border (plus a few sea links)
- US States — same idea for the 50 states and DC
- Source to Sea — follow a river from source to mouth, country by country

Play in the browser at https://www.playgeochain.uk/. It is free, with no ads and no account.
